Delete your Hop account

حذف حسابك في هوب

Hop is built by Mohamed Ayman and Youssef Amr. You can delete your Hop account and its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

Open Hop → Profile → Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: ask us

Send a message saying "Delete my Hop account", from the email address or phone number on your account, to:

Email: hop.support.eg@gmail.com
WhatsApp: +20 100 315 3562 or +20 102 509 5643

We confirm and complete the deletion within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your name, email, phone number, password, referral code, rewards, coupons, membership level and reviews are deleted or anonymized within 30 days.

What we may keep

Records of past bookings (date, field and price, without your name or contact details) may be kept for up to the period the law requires for tax and accounting. Details: Privacy Policy.
