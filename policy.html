Privacy Policy

سياسة الخصوصية · Effective 13 November 2026

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mohamed Ayman and Youssef Amr, the independent developers of Hop ("we", "us"), collect, use and protect your information when you use the Hop mobile app ("Hop" or "the app"), which lets you find and book sports fields. By using Hop, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Hop is built and run by two independent developers in Egypt: Mohamed Ayman and Youssef Amr. For any privacy question, contact us on WhatsApp at +20 100 315 3562 or +20 102 509 5643, or by email at hop.support@gmail.com.

What we don't collect: Hop does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or payment card details. Hop has no advertising or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your phone number
  • To create, show and manage your bookings
  • To share your booking with the field you booked, so they can prepare for your game
  • To apply coupons, referral rewards and membership benefits
  • To show ratings and reviews that help other players choose a field
  • To provide customer support and contact you about your bookings
  • To keep Hop secure and prevent fraud or misuse
  • To meet our legal obligations

4. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • The field you book: your name, phone number and booking details, so the field can confirm your reservation and contact you if needed.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): when you verify your number, you send a message through WhatsApp. That message is handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • Our service providers: companies that host and run our booking system for us, such as Odoo (Odoo S.A.), which hosts our booking system. They may use your information only to provide that service.
  • Other players: your rating and review of a field may be visible to other users.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or of Hop.

If you use "Share booking" or "Share referral code", you choose the app and the people you share with.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days. We may keep some booking and invoice records for longer if the law requires it, for example for tax and accounting.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. You can also email hop.support@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or message us from your phone number on WhatsApp at +20 100 315 3562 or +20 102 509 5643, and ask us to delete it.

7. Your rights

Under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020) and other applicable laws, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete your information
  • Stop using your information where you have withdrawn your consent

Contact us on WhatsApp at +20 100 315 3562 or +20 102 509 5643, or at hop.support@gmail.com, and we will reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to Egypt's Personal Data Protection Center.

8. How we protect your information

We send data between the app and our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and store your login token in your device's secure storage. We limit who on our team can access personal information. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

9. Children

Hop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app or by another suitable way before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.

4. مع مين بنشارك معلوماتك

إحنا مش بنبيع معلوماتك الشخصية. بنشاركها بس مع:

  • الملعب اللي حجزته: اسمك ورقم موبايلك وتفاصيل الحجز، عشان الملعب يأكد الحجز ويتواصل معاك لو احتاج.

  • مقدمي الخدمة: الشركات اللي بتستضيف وتشغل نظام الحجز بتاعنا، زي أودو (Odoo S.A.) اللي بيستضيف نظام الحجز بتاعنا، ومسموحلهم يستخدموا معلوماتك بس عشان يقدموا الخدمة دي.
  • اللاعيبة التانيين: تقييمك ومراجعتك للملعب ممكن يشوفها مستخدمين تانيين.
  • الجهات الرسمية: لما القانون يطلب كده، أو عشان نحمي حقوق وسلامة المستخدمين وهوب.

لو استخدمت "مشاركة الحجز" أو "مشاركة كود الدعوة"، إنت اللي بتختار التطبيق والناس اللي هتشاركهم.
